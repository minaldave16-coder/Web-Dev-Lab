About IGNITE 2026

IGNITE 2026 is our annual and only college fest, bringing together students from different backgrounds to celebrate creativity, talent, innovation, and teamwork.

The three-day event features a wide range of technical, cultural, gaming, and creative competitions, along with exciting activities, performances, and opportunities to connect with fellow students.

Whether you're here to compete, showcase your skills, or simply enjoy the experience, IGNITE 2026 has something for everyone.
