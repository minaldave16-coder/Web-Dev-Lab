🎮 Gaming Events

Step into the world of competition, strategy, and gaming through exciting gaming events at IGNITE 2026.

🏆 Esports Tournament

Teams compete in a competitive gaming tournament where strategy, teamwork, and quick decision-making are put to the test.

Rules:

  • Participants must register before the registration deadline.
  • Team size will depend on the selected game.
  • Only permitted devices and game settings may be used.
  • Unfair play or unauthorized assistance is not allowed.
  • Matches must be played according to the given schedule.

🎯 BGMI Championship

Players compete in a battle-royale tournament that tests teamwork, strategy, survival skills, and decision-making.

Rules:

  • Participants must follow the specified team format.
  • Only registered accounts may be used.
  • Unauthorized software or unfair methods are strictly prohibited.
  • Players must follow the match schedule provided by organizers.
  • The organizer's decision regarding disputes will be final.

♟️ Chess Challenge

Players compete through strategy, planning, and tactical thinking in a challenging chess competition.

Rules:

  • Participants must report before their scheduled match.
  • The tournament will follow the specified format.
  • Electronic assistance is not permitted during matches.
  • Players must follow the given time control.
  • Participants must maintain proper conduct throughout the event.

🧠 Gaming Quiz

Rules:

  • The quiz may consist of multiple rounds.
  • Questions may cover games, characters, platforms, and gaming history.
  • Each round will have a fixed time limit.

  • The coordinator's decision will be final.
