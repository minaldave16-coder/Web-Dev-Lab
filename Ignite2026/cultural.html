🎭 Cultural Events

Experience the energy, creativity, and talent of participants through exciting cultural competitions at IGNITE 2026.

💃 Dance Battle

Participants showcase their dancing skills in an energetic competition filled with creativity, rhythm, and expression.

Rules:

  • Participants can compete individually or in teams.
  • A fixed time limit will be provided for each performance.
  • Music must be submitted according to the organizer's guidelines.
  • Performances must follow the event guidelines.
  • Judging will be based on performance, creativity, synchronization, and presentation.

🎸 Battle of Bands

Musical groups compete through live performances and showcase their musical talent, coordination, and creativity.

Rules:

  • Participants must compete as a team or band.
  • Each band will receive a fixed performance time.
  • Performance material should be submitted beforehand if required.
  • Only permitted musical equipment may be used.
  • Judging will consider musical performance, coordination, creativity, and presentation.

🎭 Drama & Theatre

Teams present creative performances based on a given theme and demonstrate acting, storytelling, and stage presentation.

Rules:

  • Teams must perform according to the given theme.
  • A fixed time limit will be provided.
  • Props must follow the event guidelines.
  • Language and content must remain appropriate for the event.
  • Judging will be based on acting, creativity, storytelling, and presentation.

🎨 Art Competition

Participants express their creativity through different forms of art based on a specified theme.

Rules:

  • The artwork must be created during the competition.
  • A specific theme will be provided.
  • Only permitted materials may be used.
  • Pre-made artwork is not allowed.
  • Judging will consider creativity, originality, technique, and presentation.
