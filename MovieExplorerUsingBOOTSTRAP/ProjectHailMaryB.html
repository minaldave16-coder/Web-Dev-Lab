Project Hail Mary

Release Date: 20 March 2026

Genre: Science Fiction, Adventure

Based on: Project Hail Mary by Andy Weir

The Story

An extraterrestrial, single-celled organism called "astrophage" consumes stellar heat and drains energy from the Sun, threatening Earth with an ice age.

The Plot

Dr. Ryland Grace, a former molecular biologist turned middle-school science teacher, wakes up alone on a spaceship with amnesia.

Grace encounters Rocky, a sentient, five-limbed, mineral-shelled alien from 40 Eridani whose home star is also dying from astrophage. Together, they use the scientific method to find a biological solution.
