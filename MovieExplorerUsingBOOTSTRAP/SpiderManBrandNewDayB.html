About the Movie

Spider-Man: Brand New Day refers to a Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) movie released on July 31, 2026, as well as a famous 2008 Marvel Comics story arc.

The film is the fourth solo outing for Tom Holland's Peter Parker. It picks up four years after the events of Spider-Man: No Way Home, where the entire world forgot who Peter Parker was.

The Plot

Peter lives completely alone and fights crime full-time. The heavy stress triggers a strange physical change in his body that threatens his life, all while a mysterious telepathic threat emerges in New York City.
