About the Movie

Oppenheimer is a biographical thriller released on July 21, 2023. It is also associated with the Pulitzer Prize-winning biography American Prometheus.

The film is a historical epic directed by Christopher Nolan. It takes place during World War II, when the United States launches a top-secret race to develop the atomic bomb.

The Plot

J. Robert Oppenheimer leads a team of scientists in a secret desert location to build the first atomic bomb. The Trinity Test marks a turning point in world history, while political conflicts threaten his reputation.

Box Office Success

The film earned over $975 million worldwide and won seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture.

The Creative Team

It was written and directed by Christopher Nolan and produced by Emma Thomas.

Star-Studded Cast

Alongside Cillian Murphy and Emily Blunt, the film features Robert Downey Jr., Matt Damon, Florence Pugh, and Gary Oldman as President Harry S. Truman.
