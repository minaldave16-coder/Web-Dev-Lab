About the Movie

Avengers: Infinity War is a Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) movie released on April 27, 2018. It is the epic precursor to the Infinity Saga finale.

The film sets up the devastating events in which Thanos arrives to collect all six powerful Infinity Stones.

The Plot

The Avengers and the Guardians of the Galaxy team up in an all-out war. They must risk everything to stop the ultimate villain from wiping out half of all life in the universe.

Box Office Smash

The movie became a global box-office phenomenon, earning over $2 billion worldwide.

The Creative Team

It is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely.

Star-Studded Cast

Alongside Robert Downey Jr. and Chris Evans, the film features Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Scarlett Johansson, and Josh Brolin, who plays the villain Thanos.
