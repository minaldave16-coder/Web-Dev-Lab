About the Movie

Interstellar is a science-fiction movie released on November 7, 2014.

Directed by Christopher Nolan, Interstellar is a 169-minute science-fiction film that combines a vast cosmic adventure with an intimate family drama. Its iconic musical score was composed by Hans Zimmer.

The Plot

Set on a dying, near-future Earth devastated by widespread crop blight, the film follows former NASA pilot Joseph Cooper.

Recruited for a desperate mission to help humanity survive, Cooper leads a team through a mysterious wormhole near Saturn to search a distant galaxy for a potentially habitable world.

Box Office Success

Interstellar became a major global success and earned hundreds of millions of dollars worldwide.

The Creative Team

The film was directed by Christopher Nolan, written by Jonathan and Christopher Nolan, and produced by Emma Thomas.

Star-Studded Cast

Matthew McConaughey stars as Cooper, with Jessica Chastain as Murphy, Anne Hathaway as Brand, Timothée Chalamet as Tom, and Matt Damon as Mann.
