About the Movie

Avengers: Endgame is a Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) movie released on April 26, 2019. It is the epic finale to the 22-film Infinity Saga.

It picks up after the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, where Thanos wiped out half of all life in the universe.

The Plot

The remaining heroes live with grief and defeat. They undertake a dangerous "time heist" to gather the Infinity Stones from the past, risking their lives to bring everyone back for one final battle.

Box Office Smash

The movie became a massive global phenomenon, earning nearly $2.8 billion and becoming one of the biggest movies of all time.

The Creative Team

It is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely.

Star-Studded Cast

Alongside Robert Downey Jr. and Chris Evans, the film features Mark Ruffalo, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson, and Josh Brolin, who plays the villain Thanos.
