Solar Eclipse

A solar eclipse occurs when the Moon moves directly between Earth and the Sun, blocking all or a portion of the Sun's light from reaching our planet
1. The Core MechanicsAlignment: Happens strictly during a new moon phase when the Sun, Moon, and Earth align in a straight line (known as syzygy).
The Cosmic Coincidence: The Sun's diameter is about 400 times wider than the Moon, but the Sun is also roughly 400 times farther away from Earth. This makes both celestial bodies appear nearly identical in size from our sky.
Orbital Tilt: The Moon's orbit is tilted at a 5-degree angle relative to Earth's orbital plane around the Sun.Because of this tilt, shadows usually pass above or below Earth, which is why eclipses do not occur every single month.
