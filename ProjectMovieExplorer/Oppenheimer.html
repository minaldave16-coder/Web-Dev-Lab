The Official Poster For Oppenheimer

Oppenheimer refers to both a blockbuster biographical thriller movie released on July 21, 2023
            and a famous Pulitzer Prize-winning biography titled American Prometheus.
            The film is a highly acclaimed historical epic directed by Christopher Nolan.It picks up during World War II,
            when the United States launches a top-secret race to build the ultimate weapon.
            The Plot: J. Robert Oppenheimer leads a team of scientists in a secret desert town to build the first atomic bomb.
            The heavy stress of the Trinity Test triggers a massive shift in world history, all while a vengeful political threat emerges to ruin his reputation.
            Box Office Smash: The movie has become a massive global phenomenon,earning over $975 million and winning seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
            The Creative Team: It is directed and written by Christopher Nolan and produced by Emma Thomas.
            Star-Studded Cast: Alongside Cillian Murphy and Emily Blunt,the film features Robert Downey Jr., Matt Damon, Florence Pugh
            and Gary Oldman, who plays a brief version of President Harry S. Truman.
