Interstellar refers to a blockbuster sci-fi movie released on November 7, 2014 
            Directed by Christopher Nolan, Interstellar (2014) is a 169-minute sci-fi masterpiece that seamlessly blends massive cosmic scale with a deeply intimate family drama.
            Backed by Hans Zimmer’s iconic, organ-heavy musical score, the film stands as a masterclass in modern filmmaking.
            The PlotSet on a dying, near-future Earth ravaged by global crop blight, the film follows former NASA pilot Joseph Cooper.
            Recruited for a desperate survival mission, Cooper leads a team through a mysterious wormhole near Saturn to search a distant galaxy for a habitable new home.
            Box Office Smash: The movie has become a massive global phenomenon,earning over $975 million and winning seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
            The Creative Team: It is directed and written by Christopher Nolan and produced by Emma Thomas.
            Star-Studded Cast: Matthew mcConaughey(Cooper) , Jessica Chastain(Murphy) , anne Hathaway(Brand) , Timothee Calamet(Tom) , Matt Damon(Mann)
