About The Movie




Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness
is a Marvel Cinematic Universe movie released on May 6, 2022.
The film explores the dangerous and mysterious Multiverse.





The Plot:
Doctor Strange teams up with America Chavez, who can travel
between universes, as they face a powerful threat across the Multiverse.





Box Office Smash:
The movie became a global success, earning over $950 million worldwide.





The Creative Team:
It is directed by Sam Raimi and written by Michael Waldron.





Star-Studded Cast:
The film stars Benedict Cumberbatch, Elizabeth Olsen, Benedict Wong,
Xochitl Gomez, Chiwetel Ejiofor, and Rachel McAdams.
