About the Movie

                The Dark Knight is a 2008 superhero crime drama film directed by Christopher Nolan.
                 It is widely considered one of the greatest movies ever made. It is the second part of a three-movie series called The Dark Knight Trilogy.
                 The Plot: The movie takes place in a dark, crime-filled place called Gotham City. Three heroes team up to stop the bad guys:Batman (the masked hero)James Gordon (a clean police officer)
                 Harvey Dent (the city's brave new district attorney).Their plan works well until a new villain arrives.
                 This villain is The Joker, a scary criminal mastermind who does not care about money. He only wants to see the world burn and cause total chaos. 
                 The Joker forces Batman to make impossible choices. He tries to prove that even good people can turn evil when things get bad enough.
                 Star-Stunned Cast:Christian Bale plays Bruce Wayne / Batman, the dark hero.Heath Ledger plays The Joker, Batman's ultimate enemy.
                 Aaron Eckhart plays Harvey Dent, a good man who later turns into the villain Two-Face.Gary Oldman plays Jim Gordon, the trusted police lieutenant.
                 Michael Caine plays Alfred, Bruce Wayne's wise butler.Morgan Freeman plays Lucius Fox, the man who builds Batman's gadgets.
                 Heath Ledger's Performance: Heath Ledger played the Joker so well that he won a posthumous Academy Award (Oscar) for Best Supporting Actor after he passed away.
                 Realism: Unlike other comic book movies, this one feels like a real-world gritty crime thriller.
                 Big Success: It was a massive box office hit and earned incredibly high scores from both critics and fans on websites like IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes.
