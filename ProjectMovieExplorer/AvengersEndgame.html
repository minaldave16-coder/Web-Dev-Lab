About The Movie

Avengers : Endgame refers to both a blockbuster Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) movie originally released on April 26, 2019, 
                and a monumental event in Marvel Comics history.The film is the epic finale to the 22-film Infinity Saga.
                It picks up after the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, where Thanos wiped out half of all life in the universe.
                The Plot: The remaining heroes live with heavy grief and defeat.They pull off a dangerous "time heist" to gather the Infinity Stones from the past,
                                risking their lives to bring everyone back for one final battle.
                Box Office Smash: The movie became a massive global phenomenon,earning nearly $2.8 billion and becoming one of the biggest movies of all time.
                The Creative Team: It is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely.
                Star-Studded Cast: Alongside Robert Downey Jr. and Chris Evans,the film features Mark Ruffalo, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson, andJosh Brolin, who plays the terrifying villain Thanos.
